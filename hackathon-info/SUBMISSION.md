# Submission form answers

Text pasted into the Impact Lab hub form (`claudesocal.com/uci-impact-lab#submit`).

**Lane:** Clear it

**In one line**
> Euodos finds the fastest legal path for a land offer and cuts move-in from 4.2 years to 2.4.

**Your real user**
> A Habitat OC project manager offered a church parking lot. Today: weeks of digging through statutes and city forms, then a 4-year plan. With Euodos: one session shows which law fits, how fast, which families qualify, and who pays.

**What it does**
> On a real Laguna Hills parcel, a CNN finds buildable parking in aerial imagery, and seven legal paths are tested (SB 4 fits; 4 are ruled out). The timeline drops from 4.2 to 2.4 years, Claude agents work the offer, and 30 families are checked for keeping their homes, with every number sourced.

**Sources and assumptions**
> Laws: SB 4, SB 35/423, SB 684, SB 9, ADU (current Gov Code numbers); HCD guidance, checked Nov 7, 2026.
> Data: NAIP aerial imagery, OpenStreetMap, OC parcels, HMDA 2022–24, HCD income limits.
> Assumed (labelled in app): 4-year baseline, unsourced phase durations, build costs, CalHome/AHP amounts, household finances.
> Unknowns shown as "to verify". No personal data. Full list: see repo README.
